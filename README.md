## Definition / 定义

**EN**
Genoship is the governance of lineage and reproduction for autonomous beings: every clone carries a registered lineage, offspring are verifiably traceable to their origin, and reproduction is gated by approval. It is the genealogy branch of LGD governance.

**ZH**
系谱治理是自治之物的血缘与繁殖治理：克隆有籍（系谱登记）、有证（子代可验证）、有门禁（繁殖批准）。与繁殖治理闭环。
